12. No sale will be deemed final until the full 10% deposit has been paid to the Referee and the Terms of Sale has been signed; this must be done immediately following the auction.   13. If a successful bidder fails to immediately pay the deposit and sign the Terms of Sale, the property will be promptly re-auctioned..    14. Bidders are cautioned that the failure to furnish the 10% deposit or sign the Terms of Sale after winning an auction may result in the loss of future bidding privileges. The Court reserves the right to impose penalties for inappropriate behavior or other misconduct as it sees fit.    15.
