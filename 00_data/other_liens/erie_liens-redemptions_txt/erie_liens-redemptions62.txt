Pursuant to the provisions of the Erie County Tax Act in such cases made and provided,this List contains, as to each such parcel of real property, a brief description thereof, the name of.the last owner thereof as the same appears on the latest tax roll in the hands of the Erie County Director of Real Property Tax Services, a statement of the amount of the lien of each tax sale certificate owned by the County of Erie and unpaid on such parcel, and the certificate number and year of sale of each such tax sale certificate.    This action is brought pursuant to Article 11 of the Erie County Tax Act, and Item 11E-7(pages 404-468) adopted by the Erie County Legislature on June 26, 2025.  The parcels, which are subject to such delinquent Tax Liens and the amounts due thereon, are identified on Schedule A of this List of Delinquent Tax Liens, which is annexed hereto and  made a part hereof.
